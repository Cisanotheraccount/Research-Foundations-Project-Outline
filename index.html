RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS + PROJECT OUTLINE / FIRST DRAFT

Rent as Terrain: Making Manhattan’s Housing Pressure Tangible

Ci Song · Columbia GSAPP · Summer Colloquium 2026

Research Context Methods Experiments Plan A Plan B Drawing Sources

02 / RESEARCH QUESTIONS + KEYWORDS

How can asking-rent data become a spatial and bodily experience?

What becomes visible—and what disappears—when Manhattan is reduced to a changing terrain?

How can D3, fabrication, motion, and projection form a situated visualization practice?

HOUSING ↔ DATA PHYSICALIZATION ↔ CRITICAL CARTOGRAPHY ↔ TEMPORAL URBANISM ↔ TANGIBLE INTERACTION

03–04 / LINEAGE + COMMUNITY + SITUATION

Maps, data documents, physicalization, and tangible media form the project’s lineage.

D3 and Observable, data physicalization research, NYC housing researchers, and public data institutions form its community of practice.

Scope
Manhattan concentrates extreme rent variation in a narrow recognizable geography.
Measure
ZORI estimates asking rents rather than reporting every signed lease.
Coverage
Platform data reflects listed units and may underrepresent unavailable housing.
Abstraction
Equal hex cells aid comparison while simplifying lived geography.

05 / METHODS

One dataset moves through multiple forms.

Acquire → aggregate → map → translate → test.

Annual rent values bind to D3 marks, physical column height, printed drawing layers, and projected light.

06 / COMPUTATIONAL DESIGN EXPERIMENTS

64 cells become a temporal rent terrain.

The interactive D3 prototype maps asking-rent estimates to height and color, supports 2010–2025 playback, exposes missing data, and provides neighborhood tooltips.

Color, height, line, and warm-white material create one visual language across the website, drawing, and object.

07 / RHETORICAL ARGUMENT

Rent is not an isolated number.

It accumulates unevenly as spatial pressure.

08 / PRIMARY MATERIAL DIRECTION

Plan A: a 64-cell kinetic rent terrain.

All 64 printed hexagonal columns create the physical Manhattan field; an eight-column prototype first tests friction, synchronization, repeatability, tolerances, and motor load.

09 / ALTERNATIVE MATERIAL STRATEGY

Plan B: projection-mapped Manhattan.

A simplified white 3D-printed model receives the same rent timeline as projected color and motion.

Plan B preserves the research question and dataset if the 64-column mechanism cannot yet move reliably.

10 / DRAWING PROPOSAL

Vertical Computational Datascape / Exploded Axonometric Drawing

36 × 72 inches, with a central Manhattan terrain, 2010/2015/2020/2025 overlays, a time series, Plan A mechanical sections, Plan B projection geometry, and visible data notes.

11 / POTENTIAL CAPSTONE

From rent terrain to a housing pressure atlas.

The project may expand to five boroughs, rent-to-income, policy time, housing production, and renter testimony.

12 / THE CHALLENGE

Accuracy must survive translation.

Data coverage, mechanical friction, projection registration, and visual spectacle can each distort the project’s argument.
