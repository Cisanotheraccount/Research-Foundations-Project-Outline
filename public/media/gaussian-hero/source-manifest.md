# Gaussian Splatting hero

- `inria-bicycle-3dgs.avif`: mechanically converted from the official Inria 3D Gaussian Splatting project comparison image `content/images/comparisons/ours_bicycle.png`.
- Source: https://repo-sam.inria.fr/fungraph/3d-gaussian-splatting/
- Authors: Bernhard Kerbl, Georgios Kopanas, Thomas Leimkühler, and George Drettakis. SIGGRAPH 2023.
- The photographic region is the official 3DGS render. The browser-drawn transition on the right is an explanatory cutaway made from anisotropic, rotated, translucent ellipses sampled from the same image; it is not exported Gaussian training data.
