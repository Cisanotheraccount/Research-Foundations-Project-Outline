# Gaussian Splatting history timeline

- `mariner4-hand-colored.jpg`: resized JPEG of NASA/JPL's `PIA14033`, the first Mariner 4 digital image of Mars reconstructed from numbered paper strips and hand colored in July 1965. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/first-tv-image-of-mars-hand-colored/ Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Dan Goods.
- The 1989 Splatting panel is a code-native explanatory projection diagram based on Lee Westover's 1989 volume-rendering work; it is not a reproduced paper figure.
- The 2000 QSplat panel is a code-native explanatory multiresolution splat illustration. Stanford's statue screenshots are not reproduced because their QSplat page expressly restricts copying.
- `inria-bicycle-3dgs.jpg`: compatibility JPEG mechanically converted from the official Inria `ours_bicycle` 3DGS render recorded in `public/media/gaussian-hero/source-manifest.md`; no generative editing.
- `worldlabs-coit-tower.mp4`: official World Labs Spark 2.0 video of Vincent Woo's Coit Tower, San Francisco model (40M splats), downloaded without modification from `https://wlt-ai-cdn.art/spark-2.0/260413/videos/coit-tower.mp4`. Official page: https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/spark-2.0. The timeline's 2025 milestone refers to Marble's public launch; this visual is a later Spark 2.0 browser-rendering example.
