# World Labs interactive sample

- **Project:** Marble by World Labs.
- **World:** Ancient stone crypt, published by Evan Jones.
- **Original interactive world:** https://marble.worldlabs.ai/world/94fdf9de-939f-4d52-abe4-b09c1aafa1a6
- **Official World Labs video:** `ancient-stone-crypt-official.mp4`, downloaded without modification from `https://wlt-ai-cdn.art/videos/2025-09-16/hallway_blogwidth_opt.mp4`, as embedded in World Labs' “Generating Bigger and Better Worlds” post.
- **Local interactive asset:** `ancient-stone-crypt-500k.spz`, the public 500k Gaussian-splat export served by World Labs.
- **Local fallback:** `ancient-stone-crypt-thumbnail.webp`, the official World Labs thumbnail.
- **Use:** The official 15-second video is the primary Gaussian Splatting visual at the end of the opening sequence. The poster remains visible if video playback fails.
- **Accuracy note:** This is a Marble-generated world, not evidence of a captured 3DGS reconstruction. The interface labels it accordingly.
